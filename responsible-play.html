LUCKY VAULT · RESPONSIBLE PLAY

Responsible play

Gaming should never compromise your wellbeing or financial stability. Lucky Vault is being designed around clear information, player controls and responsible participation.

Play within your limits

  • Only participate if you meet the legal age requirement in your location.
  • Never use money needed for essentials, bills or family responsibilities.
  • Do not chase losses or borrow money to play.
  • Take breaks and stop if play is no longer enjoyable.

Controls planned for the platform

Before any real-money service is activated, the platform must provide appropriate deposit and loss limits, time-outs, self-exclusion, account closure and access to independent support resources.

If gambling is causing harm, stop and seek help from a qualified local support service. Real-money play is not enabled here.
